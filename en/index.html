Petit Planet project ↓

The barista in Petit Planet

At the café, players chat with each other and order coffee. A tricky part of building the barista was deciding who a line was meant for. She can listen while players are chatting among themselves. When someone addresses her, she has to decide whether to answer right away or work out what they mean first.

Official game test announcement (中文) ↗

Character memory

I worked on what the character can recall during a conversation and when a player comes back later.

Group dialogue

I worked on intent recognition and turn-taking, including model training and reinforcement learning. That covers decisions like who’s talking to the barista and when she needs more time to respond.

In-game actions

I worked on tool use. When the barista understands an order, she still has to make the drink in the game.

Publications
