01 / INDUSTRY RESEARCH

One AI character.
A room full of players.

  • miHoYo
  • LLM research internship
  • Petit Planet

At the café, real players share a table with an AI barista. She has to know when players are talking to each other and when they’re talking to her. A simple question can get a quick reply; an ambiguous request needs more thought. And when someone orders a coffee, she has to make it in the game.

01 / MEMORY

Remember the player

My work on core NPC memory addressed continuity across conversation turns so a character could pick up where an earlier interaction left off.

02 / DECISION

Read the room

I worked on multi-agent coordination and when to speak in group dialogue, drawing on intent recognition, model training, and reinforcement learning. Some turns call for a quick reply; others need deeper reasoning.

03 / ACTION

Turn language into action

I worked on tool use so a character could map what a player asks for to an action the game can execute, such as making a coffee.

02 / SELECTED RESEARCH

From seeing the scene to following the conversation.

My work applies LLM reasoning to game agents, visual reasoning, and group dialogue. I also follow applications to biological data and reasoning under physical constraints.

03 / SIGGRAPH ASIA 2026

Two questions for Kuala Lumpur.

At the December 3 dinner, I’d welcome the chance to compare notes with Tencent Games on two problems from multiplayer AI: what a character should remember as a world changes, and how a player’s words become an action that follows the rules of the game.

Dec 2 · Kuala Lumpur Convention Centre · MR 302/303

11:25 / VISUAL WORLD MODELS

What should a character remember when the scene changes?

Scenes change, and so do player preferences. How should character memory distinguish facts about the world from things players said and things the model inferred?

14:00 / PLAYABLE GENERATIVE WORLDS

How does a sentence become a reliable game action?

Take a simple coffee order: when should an agent ask for clarification, and how can it check that its next action fits the current state and the rules of play?
